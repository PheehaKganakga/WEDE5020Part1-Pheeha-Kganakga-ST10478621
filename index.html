Our Purpose

The Kganakga Foundation exists to share updates, events, community programs, and opportunities from Sandfontein Village in Rustenburg. Our key goals include:

  • Create jobs for local residents
  • Promote youth empowerment
  • Drive agricultural innovation
  • Improve village infrastructure

Our Audience

We aim to reach:

  • Residents of Sandfontein Village
  • Potential donors and partners
  • NGOs and volunteers
  • Professionals who want to contribute

Research & Content Gathering

To ensure authenticity, we interview community members for stories and testimonials about development in the village. We also draw inspiration from organizations like the Lima Rural Development Foundation, which supports the simultaneous development of people and livelihoods in resource-scarce rural contexts.
